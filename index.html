      Welcome to Velocity Circuit! 🏁

      A quick rundown before your first race — you can revisit all of this any time from Settings.

      🎮 Driving

      Keyboard: W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys to drive, Shift to drift, Space to boost. On a touchscreen you'll get an on-screen joystick plus drift/boost buttons. Plug in a gamepad and it's picked up automatically. Every one of these can be remapped later in Settings.

      🌀 Drift boost

      Hold a drift through a corner and it charges a mini-turbo — the on-screen indicator grows (and changes color) as it builds through three tiers, then release for a speed boost.

      🌦️ Weather

      Some tracks have rain, snow, ash, or fog — grip, braking, and engine power all shift with conditions, the same for every racer. Rain and snow can also throw a crosswind gust or a hidden puddle/ice patch at you, and thick fog or ashfall cuts how far ahead you can see.

      🎨 Garage & Upgrades

      Earn coins by racing, then spend them on new cars, paint, tires, and Engine Tunes in the Garage, or on performance upgrades in the Upgrades shop.

      Click a track to start racing instantly. Controls: W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys to drive. Shift to drift. Esc to pause. C to chat. Complete laps to earn coins — spend them on new cars in the Garage and performance upgrades in the Upgrades shop, and finish races to unlock harder tracks. CPU racers adapt to your skill in real time and cars can collide, so watch your line into corners.

        Championships

        Race a fixed circuit of tracks back-to-back. Each finish earns championship points (12/8/5/3 for 1st–4th) that carry over between races — the bigger your total by the final stage, the bigger the coin payout.

        Track Editor

        Click empty space to add a point, drag a point to move it, click a point again to remove it. Add at least 4 points — the loop closes automatically and the finish line sits at your first (green) point.
